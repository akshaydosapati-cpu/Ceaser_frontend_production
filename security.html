Overview

CEASER is built on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure designed with security, privacy, and reliability at its core.

Authentication, storage, databases, and AI services are powered by trusted technology partners that maintain internationally recognized security programs, allowing CEASER to deliver a secure and privacy-first experience from day one.

Enterprise Infrastructure

CEASER is powered by globally trusted cloud platforms including:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, and secure storage
  • Render — Secure backend infrastructure
  • OpenAI — AI intelligence platform

These infrastructure providers maintain internationally recognized security programs, including ISO 27001 and SOC 2 certifications where applicable, ensuring CEASER is built on a secure foundation.

Authentication

CEASER uses secure account authentication and user-scoped sessions. Product data is associated with authenticated users so projects, memories, files, documents, integrations, and generated outputs remain connected to the correct account.

Data Encryption

Communication between CEASER clients and backend services is protected using HTTPS/TLS. Database connections and managed storage are handled through cloud providers that support encrypted transport and operational security controls.

Responsible AI

CEASER follows a privacy-first AI architecture.

Only the information required to complete your request is securely processed by the configured AI provider. Personal projects, memories, and knowledge remain isolated to your account, and AI context is built only from the data necessary to fulfil your request.

Data Ownership

You own your data. CEASER never sells your personal information and only processes the data necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

Security Standards

CEASER's launch security foundation includes:

  • Enterprise-grade infrastructure
  • Encrypted communications
  • Secure authentication
  • User-isolated workspaces
  • Private document storage
  • Knowledge Engine access controls
  • Continuous monitoring
  • Responsible AI processing

Enterprise Roadmap

Enterprise customers often require additional governance capabilities. As CEASER scales, our roadmap includes:

  • Advanced audit logging
  • Enterprise SSO
  • Enhanced compliance support
  • Governance improvements
  • Expanded security controls

Responsible Disclosure

If you believe you have found a security issue in CEASER, please report it responsibly and avoid accessing, modifying, or sharing user data. We will review credible reports and prioritize fixes based on severity.
